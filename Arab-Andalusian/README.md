# Arab-Andalusian Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Arab-Andalusian music (including the Moroccan *al-Âla*, Algerian *Gharnati*, and Tunisian *Ma'lūf* traditions).

## 📖 Overview
Arab-Andalusian music is an oral tradition organized into complex suites called *Nawba*. It relies on specific melodic modes (*Ṭāb'*) and rhythmic cycles (*Mīzān*), utilizing characteristic melodic figures (*centones*) and diatonic or microtonal inflections that distinguish it from both Western classical and Eastern Arab (*Maqam*) traditions. This repository serves as a central hub for researchers focusing on computational approaches to this Maghrebi and Andalusian heritage.

## 🗄 Datasets & Corpora
Links to the primary verified data sources used in the field.

| Dataset | Type | Description | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arab-Andalusian Music Corpus** | Audio/Score | A large-scale corpus of 164 concert recordings, manual transcriptions, and metadata collected by the CompMusic project. | [Zenodo Archive](https://zenodo.org/records/1291776) |
| **Arab-Andalusian Lyrics Dataset** | Text | Time-aligned lyrics (*san'a*) corresponding to the CompMusic Arab-Andalusian corpus. | [Zenodo Archive](https://zenodo.org/records/1291904) |
| **Dunya Arab-Andalusian** | Audio/Metadata | A web-based corpus exploration tool and audio collection by CompMusic. | [Dunya Portal](https://dunya.compmusic.upf.edu/arab_andalusian/) |
| **ORD-CC32 (Cairo Congress)** | Audio/Metadata | Open research dataset of the 1932 Cairo Congress of Arab Music, including early historical recordings of Maghrebi and Andalusian suites. | [ArXiv / GitHub](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14503) |
