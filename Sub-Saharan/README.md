# Sub-Saharan African Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Sub-Saharan African music traditions.

## 📖 Overview
Sub-Saharan African music is characterized by dense polyrhythmic structures, interlocking timeline patterns (such as standard bell patterns), non-isochronous micro-timing, and call-and-response forms. Because rhythm in these traditions is often organized around cyclical timelines rather than Western hierarchical downbeats, and because melodies are heavily influenced by tonal languages and unique tuning systems (like the Ugandan *Amadinda* or Mozambican *Timbila*), standard MIR algorithms often fail to generalize here. This repository centralizes research dedicated to decolonizing AI and developing culturally grounded models for African music.
