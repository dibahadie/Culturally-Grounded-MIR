# Carnatic Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of South Indian (Carnatic) Classical music.

## 📖 Overview
Carnatic music is built upon the *Raga* melodic framework and *Tala* rhythmic cycles, distinguished by intricate microtonal pitch oscillations (*Gamakas*), rapid syllabic percussion (*Solkattu* / *Konnakol*), and structured compositional forms (*Kriti*, *Varnam*). Because notes (*Swaras*) are defined by their characteristic continuous pitch trajectories rather than static frequencies, standard Western MIR algorithms require specialized melodic and rhythmic modeling. This repository serves as a central hub for researchers to find papers and datasets.

## 🗄 Datasets & Corpora
Links to the primary verified data sources used in the field.

| Dataset | Type | Description | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Saraga (Carnatic)** | Audio/Annotations | Open dataset of Carnatic concert recordings with time-aligned tonic, pitch, rhythm, and structural annotations by CompMusic. | [MTG GitHub](https://mtg.github.io/saraga/) |
| **Dunya Carnatic** | Audio/Metadata | A web-based corpus exploration tool and audio collection by CompMusic. | [Dunya Portal](https://dunya.compmusic.upf.edu/carnatic/) |
| **Carnatic Varnam Dataset** | Audio/Annotations | Annotated multi-track recordings of Carnatic *Varnams* for structural and melodic analysis. | [CompMusic Archive](https://compmusic.upf.edu/node/326) |
| **Mridangam Stroke Dataset** | Audio | Annotated recordings of individual and sequenced *Mridangam* percussion strokes for timbre and rhythm modeling. | [CompMusic Archive](https://compmusic.upf.edu/node/330) |
