# Flamenco Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Andalusian *Flamenco* music.

## 📖 Overview
Flamenco is an improvisational oral tradition rooted in Andalusia, Spain, combining expressive vocal singing (*cante*), acoustic guitar accompaniment (*toque*), and percussive hand-clapping (*palmas*) and dance (*baile*). Key computational challenges include automatic transcription of heavily ornamented, microtonally inflected vocal melodies (*cante*), style (*palo*) classification, and modeling complex 12-beat rhythmic cycles (*compás*).
