# Balkan & Byzantine Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Balkan, Greek traditional, and Byzantine chant music.

## 📖 Overview
Balkan and Greek traditional music are renowned for non-isochronous, asymmetric meters (*aksak*, such as 7/8 and 9/8), modal scales, and regional instrumental idioms, while Byzantine chant employs its own neumatic notation system and microtonal modal framework (*echos*). Standard Western MIR algorithms built around isochronous 4/4 meters and 12-TET harmony frequently struggle with these rhythmic and melodic structures.
