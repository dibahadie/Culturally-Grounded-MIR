# Celtic & Nordic Folk Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Celtic (Irish, Scottish) and Nordic (Swedish, Norwegian) traditional folk music.

## 📖 Overview
Celtic and Nordic traditional dance music—such as Irish jigs and reels, and Swedish *polska* (*slängpolska*)—rely heavily on modal scales (Dorian, Mixolydian, Ionian, Aeolian), bipartite tune structures, oral transmission, and expressive rhythmic micro-timing (such as asymmetrical triple-meter beats in Scandinavian *polska*). In MIR, these traditions are widely studied through large community-curated ABC notation corpora and symbolic generative modeling.
