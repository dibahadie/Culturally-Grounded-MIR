# Japanese Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Japanese traditional music, including *Gagaku* (imperial court music) and *Shakuhachi*.

## 📖 Overview
Japanese traditional music encompasses unmetered and elastic timing structures (such as *jobuki* in *Gagaku* and the concept of *ma*), complex breath and timbral articulations (*Shakuhachi*), and specialized vertical or tablature-based notation systems (*hichiriki*, *koto*, and *shamisen* notations). Because open-access MIR corpora for Japanese traditional music remain relatively scarce compared to Western or contemporary J-Pop datasets, this collection highlights foundational computational studies and encoding efforts.
