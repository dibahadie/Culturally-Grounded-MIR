# Korean Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Korean traditional music (*Gugak*), including *Pansori*, *Jeongak*, *Min'yo* (folk songs), and *Jeongganbo* mensural notation.

## 📖 Overview
Korean traditional music (*Gugak*) is shaped by three core structural elements: rhythmic cycles (*jangdan*), melodic lines (*garak*), and expressive continuous pitch inflections and ornamentations (*sigimsae*), alongside regional modal identities (*tori*) and traditional grid-based mensural notation (*Jeongganbo*). Because *sigimsae* and *tori* cannot be reduced to static 12-TET pitches, computational research in *Gugak* focuses on continuous pitch-contour modeling, self-supervised representation learning, and optical/symbolic recognition of traditional notation.

## 🗄 Datasets & Corpora
Links to the primary verified data sources used in the field.

| Dataset | Type | Description | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gugak Acoustic Descriptor Corpus** | Audio | A 3.6 GB segment-level dataset of Korean traditional music (*Gugak*) curated for MIR and digital heritage analysis. | [Zenodo Archive](https://zenodo.org/records/19964647) |
| **Gugak-VocalSet** | Audio/Annotations | A studio-quality corpus of 305 unaccompanied Korean traditional vocal tracks (24.5 hours, including *Pansori* and *Jeongga*) with ~68,000 audio-based *sigimsae* annotations. | [ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15350) |
| **Jeongganbo / Jeongak Dataset** | Symbolic/Notation | Machine-readable dataset of traditional Korean *Jeongganbo* mensural notation curated for computational *Gugak* research. | [Project Page](https://www.danbinaerin.com/Jeongganbo_dataset/) |
