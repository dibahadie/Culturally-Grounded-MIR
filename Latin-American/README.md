# Latin American Music Information Retrieval & Generation Paper Collection

A curated, comprehensive collection of research papers, datasets, and computational resources for the analysis, recognition, and generative modeling of Latin American music traditions, including Uruguayan *Candombe*, Brazilian *Samba*, *Choro*, and *MPB*, Colombian *Bambuco*, and regional Latin genres.

## 📖 Overview
Latin American music encompasses an extraordinarily diverse range of Afro-Atlantic, Indigenous/Andean, and Iberian-influenced traditions. Key computational challenges include non-isochronous microtiming and groove, Afro-Atlantic percussive timelines, beat/downbeat accentuation that diverges from Western pop hierarchies (such as *Samba* and *Candombe*), bi-metric *sesquialtera* (3/4 vs. 6/8 in Colombian *Bambuco*), and low-resource dataset curation.

## 🗄 Datasets & Corpora
Links to the primary verified data sources used in the field.

| Dataset | Type | Description | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Uruguayan Candombe Drumming Dataset** | Audio/Annotations | Over 2 hours of studio recordings (35 complete performances by 3–5 drummers) with >4,700 expert-annotated downbeats. | [Zenodo Archive](https://zenodo.org/records/6533068) |
| **BRID (Brazilian Rhythmic Instruments Dataset)** | Audio/Annotations | 367 tracks (274 solo, 93 multi-instrument) covering 10 Brazilian percussion classes across 5 rhythmic styles (*samba*, *partido alto*, *samba-enredo*, *capoeira*, *marcha*). | [Zenodo Archive](https://zenodo.org/records/14051323) |
| **ACMUS-MIR (Andean Colombian Music)** | Audio/Annotations | Expert-annotated dataset of Andean Colombian music covering rhythm/meter (*sesquialtera* in *bambuco*), instrumental format, and scale subsets. | [Zenodo Archive](https://zenodo.org/record/3829091) |
| **The MPB Corpus** | Symbolic/Annotations | A dataset of Brazilian Popular Music (*MPB*) encoding melodic contour, rhythm, harmony, and melody–harmony relationships. | [ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13842v1) / [TISMIR](https://doi.org/10.5334/tismir.357) |
| **Latin Music Database (LMD)** | Audio/Metadata | Benchmark collection of Latin musical recordings across 10 genres (including Tango, Samba, Salsa, and Bolero) designed for automatic genre classification. | [ISMIR 2008 Paper](https://ismir2008.ismir.net/papers/ISMIR2008_106.pdf) |
